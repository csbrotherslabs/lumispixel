Our Accessibility Commitment

We want every visitor to explore LumisPixel with confidence, regardless of ability, technology, or context. Accessibility is considered throughout our design and development process.

Our team works toward clear, consistent experiences and reviews improvements as the platform grows. We will update this page as our practices and capabilities evolve.

02

Accessibility Features

Our interface is designed around familiar patterns, clear feedback, and flexible ways to navigate. The following areas guide our current accessibility work.

Keyboard Navigation

Move through key controls and page content without relying on a mouse.

Screen Reader Support

Semantic structure and descriptive labels help assistive technology interpret content.

High Contrast Design

Readable color combinations help important text and controls remain distinct.

Responsive Layouts

Content adapts across desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes.

Clear Typography

Comfortable sizing, spacing, and hierarchy make information easier to scan.

Accessible Forms

Helpful labels, instructions, and validation support successful form completion.

Feature coverage may vary while we continue reviewing older and newly released experiences.

03

Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing responsibility rather than a one-time project. We regularly review navigation, page structure, forms, color use, and interaction patterns.

As new tools are introduced, we aim to test them with a range of devices and assistive technologies. Priorities may be refined in response to user feedback.

04

Supported Browsers and Devices

LumisPixel is designed for current versions of widely used browsers on desktop and mobile devices. Keeping your browser and operating system updated generally provides the best experience.

Behavior can vary across browser, device, and assistive technology combinations. If something does not work as expected, please tell us what setup you are using.

05

Known Limitations

Some older pages, third-party integrations, or complex photography tools may not yet provide the same level of accessibility as the rest of the platform.

We are working to identify and address these gaps. When an immediate fix is not available, our support team will try to help you find another way to complete your task.

06

Feedback and Assistance

Your experience helps us understand where the platform can improve. We welcome reports about barriers, confusing interactions, or content that is difficult to access.

When possible, include the page, feature, device, browser, and assistive technology involved. This context helps our team investigate efficiently.

07

Contact Us

Contact LumisPixel if you need help accessing information or completing an action. We will work with you to understand your request and identify an appropriate option.

Accessibility feedback can be submitted through our contact page. Please include your preferred way for our team to respond.
