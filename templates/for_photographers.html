A day in the life

From booking to print purchase, the story stays connected.

Morning starts with a new client booking request. You create the event in LumisPixel, shoot the session, and upload the finished images when you return to the studio.

While you recharge, AI processes the gallery, groups people, flags quality issues, and makes the event searchable. The client uploads a selfie, finds their photos instantly, chooses favorites, downloads approved images, and buys prints.

By the next morning, revenue, downloads, gallery views, popular images, and engagement insights have already updated—without another spreadsheet.
