Common questions

Partner FAQ

Everything you need to take the next step.

Who can become a partner?

Technology companies, educators, associations, resellers, affiliates, and businesses that support photographers are welcome to apply.

Is there a partner program fee?

There is no standard application fee. Any program-specific terms will be shared clearly during review.

Do partners receive marketing resources?

Yes. Approved partners can receive brand guidance, campaign materials, and launch support suited to their program.

Can technology companies integrate with LumisPixel?

Yes. We welcome integration ideas that create smoother, more connected workflows for photographers.

How do I apply?

Select “Become a Partner,” share a few details, and our partner team will follow up.
