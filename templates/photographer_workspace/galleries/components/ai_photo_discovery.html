AI Photo Discovery

Help your clients find their photos using AI. Semantic and facial search have separate permissions, so you can choose which features to enable for this gallery.

Both features are off by default for new galleries until their policies and safeguards are approved.

Semantic Photo Search

Let clients search photos using natural language descriptions.

  • Search by scenes, objects, and descriptions
  • Works across authorized photos in this gallery
  • Respects gallery visibility and client permissions
  • Does not identify individuals by face
Example searches
“first dance”“birthday cake”“bride and groom”“dancing guests”“sunset portraits”“ceremony”
Privacy

Semantic search uses image content to find relevant photos. It does not identify or match individuals by face.

Find My Photos (Facial Search)

Let clients use a selfie to find photos containing their face.

  • Uses facial recognition to find matching photos
  • Requires verified permissions and legal approval
  • Managed independently from semantic search
  • Only returns photos clients are authorized to access
Requires approval

Facial indexing must be enabled only after required permissions and platform safeguards are verified.

Biometric data

Facial search processes biometric information. Obtain required consent and follow applicable privacy laws.
