Privacy First

LumisPixel is committed to protecting photographer and client data while providing secure AI-powered services.

01

Introduction

This policy describes the information LumisPixel may process when you visit our sites, create an account, or use our photography services.

It is intended as a clear overview and will be updated with attorney-reviewed details as our services evolve.

02

Information We Collect

We may collect account details, contact information, payment-related records, device data, and information you provide when contacting us.

We may also process photographs, gallery activity, preferences, and other content submitted through the service.

03

How We Use Your Information

We use information to provide, maintain, personalize, and improve LumisPixel, and to communicate about accounts and support requests.

Information may also help us secure the platform, understand product performance, and meet legal obligations.

04

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the service working, remember preferences, and understand site usage.

Available controls and choices may vary by browser, device, and location.

05

AI Features and Uploaded Images

When enabled, AI features may analyze uploaded images to support search, organization, culling, editing, or other requested functions.

Photographers are responsible for having appropriate rights and permissions before uploading content. Additional feature-specific notices may apply.

06

Sharing Information with Third Parties

We may share limited information with vendors that support hosting, payments, analytics, communications, and other platform operations.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a properly notified business transaction.

07

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to safeguard personal information.

No storage or transmission system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide services, support legitimate business purposes, and satisfy legal requirements.

Retention periods may depend on the type of information, account settings, contractual needs, and applicable law.

09

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of certain personal information.

You may also have choices about marketing and some processing activities. We may need to verify requests before responding.

10

Children's Privacy

LumisPixel is not directed to children who cannot legally consent to the processing of their information.

Account holders uploading images of children are responsible for obtaining any permissions required by applicable law.

11

International Users

Information may be processed in countries other than the country where it was collected.

Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international data transfers and provide applicable privacy rights.

12

Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy to reflect changes to LumisPixel, our practices, or applicable requirements.

We will post the updated policy here and provide additional notice when a material change requires it.

13

Contact Us

Contact LumisPixel if you have questions about this policy, our privacy practices, or a request involving your information.

Please include enough detail for us to identify the relevant account or service and respond appropriately.
