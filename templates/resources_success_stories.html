From Scattered Client Tasks to One Connected Wedding Workflow

Business profile

An independent wedding photographer managing inquiries, bookings, questionnaires, timelines, contracts, galleries, and follow-up across several disconnected tools.

Challenge

Important information was spread across email, spreadsheets, calendar notes, files, and separate gallery tools. Repeated follow-up made busy-season work harder to manage.

LumisPixel approach

This illustrative workflow connects client records, booking details, agreements, payment steps, preparation, gallery access, image delivery, and follow-up within a more unified process.

Possible outcome

The example shows how a photographer could reduce repeated handoffs, improve workflow visibility, and create a more consistent client experience.
